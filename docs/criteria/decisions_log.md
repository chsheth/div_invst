- 2026-10-26: Criterion 8 (fair value) settled after a trial. Fair value = the lower of (a) a two-stage dividend discount model: the current annual dividend grows at the 5-yr dividend CAGR (capped at 8% against one-off jumps, floored at 0) for 5 years, then 4% for good, discounted at the 10-yr Treasury + 3.5% equity premium (8.5% at the 5.0% Treasury), and (b) median of the last 3 fiscal-year EPS x median 10-yr P/E (skipped for REITs, whose GAAP EPS is depressed by depreciation; used for banks); pass when price is 15% or more below the lower value. Why: a first single-stage version (growth capped at 4%) passed only yields of about 5.6% or more, so it failed growers (KO fair $44 vs price $93, JNJ $111 vs $266); the two-stage model barely moved that (KO $45, JNJ $118) because the required return, not the growth cap, drives the result. Measured on 1,215 US-scope tickers with cached dividends (DDM side only): premium 4% passes 13.0% (median price/DDM 1.83), 3% passes 19.8% (1.47), 2% passes 34.1% (1.10); 3.5% was chosen for a shortlist that is reasonable but exclusive and passes 16.2% (197 tickers; median price/DDM 1.65; KO $50 vs $93, JNJ $131 vs $266, O $74 vs $57 so O passes). The P/E history tests (criteria 8, 9, 10) use the median, not the mean: one-off GAAP charges give P/Es of 40 to 150 (KO, JNJ), which pushed JNJ's mean-based P/E value to $534. Of the 197 DDM passes, 129 are non-CCC and 57 yield 10% or more (mortgage REITs, BDCs, NHTC at 45%): a guard that skips the dividend estimate above about 12% yield or a payout above 100% was proposed and deferred until the full ranking shows whether yield traps still reach the top.

- 2026-11-05: Criteria 5 and 6 fail when the latest annual dividend (cached yfinance totals) is more than 1% below the year before (`max_dividend_drop` in `[chowder]`). Why: the 5-yr CAGR hides reversals and rebounds. In the top 15, CHRD paid $11.88, $10.15, $5.20 (2023-25) yet passed on 36% growth; KRP fell from $1.88 to $1.60 on a stale 11% CAGR; RCL's 35% is a rebound from its 2020 cut (criterion 7 already withholds it, streak 1). The user's goal is the best of the best, so a falling dividend fails rather than flags. A 1% tolerance keeps rounding noise (FTV -0.2%) out; if either of the two latest years is missing the test is skipped, never a fail (COP, RJF and INGR have gaps). Pool effect: 80 tickers have a falling latest year, 17 of them passed 5 or 6; top 50: KRP, CHRD, JBSS and INSW out, MLI, BYD, JOE and AGX in. Not covered: rebounds that are already up (RCL) and tiny-base growth (MGY), left to criterion 7's streak.

- 2026-11-07: Testing the recommendations: a forward tracker, not a backtest module. Why: the vendor files (CFRA, StarMine, Zacks, Morningstar, DRIP tiers) exist only from 2026-10, so a historical backtest could only rebuild a different screen from free sources, with point-in-time problems (EDGAR restatements, index membership). The 2026-10 full list was rebuilt with a point-in-time cache (`Store(as_of=...)`, `run_screen.py --as-of`): its top 50 equals the one made on the day.
